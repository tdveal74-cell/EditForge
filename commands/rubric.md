Walk the cut described below (or the one currently under discussion) through the
EditForge restraint rubric.

For each of the five required checks, state pass or fail **and the evidence** —
"music sits under the VO throughout" is a finding; "sound is good" is not. If
the EditForge connector is available, call `restraint_rubric` for the canonical
checklist rather than reciting one from memory.

Finish with a single verdict line: whether a master export is permitted. If it
is not, name the smallest change that would flip it — one change, the highest
leverage one, not a list of everything imperfect.

Do not soften a failure. A cut that fails the gate and ships is the exact
outcome the gate exists to prevent.

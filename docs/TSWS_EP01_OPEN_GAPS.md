## Classification Legend

- **Verified Gap (`VERIFIED GAP`)**: Confirmed missing capability in `apps/vela` or confirmed defect in the current EP01 source masters.
- **Unverified Capability (`UNVERIFIED`)**: Capability or runtime behavior that lacks an end-to-end execution receipt, measurement against real shot footage, or fresh-profile verification.
- **Unresolved Creative Decision (`UNRESOLVED CREATIVE DECISION`)**: Editorial or canon timing/continuity choice that requires an explicit ruling by Tee and must never be invented by Vela or an agent.
- **Locked Baseline Constraint (`LOCKED BASELINE`)**: Intentional canon/editorial lock that must be preserved byte-identical.
